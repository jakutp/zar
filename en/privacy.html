ZAR Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026 • Version 2026-10-17 • Terms of Use • Türkçe

Data controller: Kutay Türker (developer of ZAR). Contact: github.com/jakutp/zar/issues. This policy is published in Turkish and English; if they conflict, the Turkish text prevails.

In short

ZAR has no server of its own, no ads and no analytics or tracking tools. You can use every suggestion/discovery feature of the app as a guest, without creating an account; in that mode we do not collect or store your personal data. To create suggestions, the app sends location coordinates directly from your device to the open data services listed below, which process the request together with your IP address and browser information under their own policies. Optionally, you can create an account and submit completed activities for approval with a proof photo; in that case your account and activity data are stored on Google's Firebase infrastructure — see "Account system, photo approval and Firebase" below.

Location

If you grant location permission, your device location is read only while the app is open and when you start a search. If you do not, you can pick a location on the map or by typing an address. The last location you chose is stored only on your device for the next launch.

Account system, photo approval and Firebase

Creating an account is entirely optional; if you don't, the app keeps working exactly as it does without one. If you create an account (with e-mail and password), we keep your authentication and data on Google's Firebase infrastructure (Authentication, Firestore database, Storage file store — servers in Europe and the US); this means Google acts as a sub-processor. Your password never reaches us — it goes straight to Google's authentication system, which we cannot see.

When you mark an activity "completed" and upload a proof photo: the photo, that activity's place information, your GPS location at the moment of completion, and a timestamp are saved to Firebase and enter "pending approval" status. This record is reviewed — for now manually, and in the future optionally with an automated visual check — by the app's developer (the data controller), who approves or rejects it; if approved, you receive your discovery points. During this review the developer sees your photo, the place information and your location at that time. This data is kept until you delete your account or separately ask for it to be removed.

You can permanently delete your account at any time via Settings → Account → delete my account; this removes your profile, all activity-approval records and the photos you uploaded from Firebase (your local discovery points/history on-device are kept separately and can be cleared from Settings). Account and activity data (approved/rejected records and photos) are kept for as long as your account remains open, because keeping your points and activity history with you is the purpose of this service. When you delete your account, or ask us to delete a single record, the data is removed from the live system immediately and permanently deleted from Google's backups within 30 days at most. Where a legal retention obligation applies (e.g. a request from a public authority), the relevant data is kept only for that period and with restricted access. Using Google Cloud/Firebase automatically brings you under Google's Cloud Data Processing Addendum, included in their standard terms of service; no separately signed document is required. See Firebase Privacy and Security for Firebase's own sub-processors and data handling.

Stored on your device

The app keeps the following in local storage (localStorage and IndexedDB): last location, theme and settings, a temporary cache of service responses (up to a few weeks), preference weights learned from the suggestions you accept or skip, your Gemini API key if you entered one, your discovery points/level, your terms and consent records (including the device identifier), and, if you created an account, Firebase's own session information (so it can recognise you without signing in again). Other than your account/session info, none of this is sent off your device. Deleting the app or clearing its data removes all of it (if you have an account, your Firebase data must also be removed separately via "delete my account").

These permissions are optional; refusing them does not prevent you from using the app. Your data is not used for any purpose you do not agree to. Advertising (planned: Google AdMob)

This version shows no ads and contains no advertising SDK. A future version is planned to add Google's ad service Google AdMob (Google Ireland Ltd. / Google LLC) to keep the app free. Before that version ships, this section will be finalised, the App Store privacy label updated, and your choices asked again at launch. Planned behaviour:

  • If you answer "yes" to ads: when ads are shown, Google receives your IP address (including approximate location derived from it), device and app information (model, OS, language), ad interactions (impressions, clicks) and — only if you also allow iOS tracking (App Tracking Transparency) — your device's advertising identifier (IDFA). Without tracking permission, ads are shown non-personalised, without the advertising identifier.
  • If you answer "no" to ads: the advertising SDK is not started at all and no data is sent to Google for advertising.
  • Your GPS location, account details, photos and activity history are never shared for advertising.
  • For users aged 13–18 and those who have not stated their age, only non-personalised ads are requested.
  • Google processes this data under its own policy and outside Türkiye (EU and US): How Google uses information from partner apps. The transfer abroad relies on your explicit consent (KVKK Art. 9); you can withdraw it at any time in Settings.

Statement of explicit consent

On first launch the following statement is confirmed: "I have read, understood and accept the Terms of Use and the Privacy Policy. I give my explicit consent to the processing of my personal data for the purposes I answer 'yes' to." Accepting the terms is required; consent to optional purposes is given freely and separately for each purpose.

Consent record

Who consented to which purpose, when, and under which version of the texts (or withdrew consent) is kept on your device in an append-only log, with a random installation identifier and, on iOS, Apple's identifier for vendor (identifierForVendor; this is not the advertising identifier and is not used for tracking), the app version and language, and without your name or contact details. You can view and export this record in Settings. If an account or server is added in the future, the record may also be stored on the server under this identifier for evidence purposes; this will be stated in this policy in advance.

Legal bases

Your location is processed to provide the suggestion service (performance of a contract, Turkish Personal Data Protection Law (KVKK) art. 5/2-c). Optional purposes are processed only with your explicit consent (KVKK art. 5/1). Creating an account and the activity-approval flow are likewise a contractual relationship you initiate yourself (KVKK art. 5/2-c): entering your e-mail/password and tapping "sign up", and uploading and submitting a photo, are explicit acts and consent. The app is currently not offered on the App Store in the European Union or mainland China.

Children

ZAR is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal data from anyone. When creating an account you represent that you are over 13; if you are 13–17, parental/guardian consent is required (see Terms of Use). If we learn that someone under 13 has created an account, we delete the account and its associated data; please report this at github.com/jakutp/zar/issues if you notice it.

Your rights and contact

Under KVKK art. 11 you have the right to learn whether your data is processed, and to request correction, deletion, objection and withdrawal of consent. Because your data is kept on your device, deleting the app data removes all records; you can export your consent record from within the app. Questions and requests: github.com/jakutp/zar/issues. For data held by third-party services, see their policies: OSMF, Wikimedia, Open-Meteo, Google.

Changes

If this policy changes, this page, the date and the version above are updated. For changes affecting optional permissions, the app asks for your consent again at launch; consent given earlier does not count for a new purpose.
