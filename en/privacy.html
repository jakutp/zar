ZAR Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026 • Version 2026-10-29 • Terms of Use • Türkçe

Data controller: Kutay Türker (ZAR developer). Contact: github.com/jakutp/zar/issues. If the Turkish and English texts conflict, the Turkish text prevails.

In short

When you can't decide what to do, ZAR puts six real places near you on the faces of a die. Your request and location are sent to the ZAR server, which runs on Google's Firebase/Google Cloud infrastructure in Europe (europe-west1); it finds places from Google Maps (Places) data, falling back to open data (Overture Maps, OpenStreetMap), and picks them with AI (Google Gemini). Without an account you only see a summary of your request; results, points, neighbourhood rankings, events and the business panel need a free account. We do not write your search text or location to server logs and never use them for advertising.

Location

If you grant location permission, your location is read only while the app is open: when you search, or when you confirm that you reached a place or an event. Otherwise you can pick a location on the map or by address. Your location is sent to the server to find nearby places and is not stored. The location of the six places on your die is kept for 24 hours so we can verify a visit, then deleted. When you complete a task, your GPS position is used only to check whether you are near the place.

Account, nickname and community

When signing up you choose a Personal or Business account. Others see only your chosen nickname, level, points, home neighbourhood and visit/task/check-in counts (neighbourhood ranking and mini profile). Your email is never shown. Your Instagram handle is visible only if you turn on "show on my profile". Events you create (title, description, place, time, nickname) are public; your nickname on events you join is visible only to the organiser. Event texts are moderated automatically (word filter and Gemini); suspicious content is reviewed before publishing. You can block users and report events and users; we review reports.

Points and location checks

Points are awarded by the server when your location is close enough to a place from your die, or when you are at an event during its time. For photo tasks your photo is sent to Gemini, which assesses whether it is a real photo, matches the expected place type and contains inappropriate content; combined with GPS distance, a clear match is approved automatically, inappropriate content is rejected and borderline cases are reviewed by the developer. Only this system and the developer see your photo. We do not try to identify faces.

Business accounts

Business name, category, address, location, phone, website, contact email and tax/legal name are reviewed by the developer for verification; the name, address and location of an approved business are public in sponsored suggestions and events. Business panel statistics (impressions, picks, verified visits, check-ins) are counts only; businesses never receive users' identities or locations.

Tanış (coming soon, pre-registration only for now)

Tanış is a separate section being built for users 18 and over to meet nearby people or find a company for outings; it is not live in this version. From the "tanış" tab on the neighbourhood screen you can currently only pre-register: we store your account identity and your declaration that you are 18 or older on the ZAR server (Firestore), so we can notify you when it launches. Pre-registration does not ask for your location, gender or who you want to meet, is never shown to anyone, and is not used for matching. When Tanış actually launches, we will ask for a full, separately and explicitly consented registration — including special-category data such as who you want to meet — and this page will be updated then. We delete your pre-registration automatically when you delete your account, or on request.

Ads (Google AdMob)

  • Ads are rewarded and optional: if you choose to watch, you earn an extra search or weekly points. Ad points never count towards any ranking.
  • If you say "yes" to ads: when you watch an ad, your IP address (including approximate location), device and app information, ad interactions and —only if you also allow iOS tracking (App Tracking Transparency)— your advertising ID (IDFA) are transferred to Google. Without tracking permission ads are non-personalised. To confirm a reward, Google sends the ZAR server a transaction ID and your ZAR user ID.
  • If you say "no": the ad SDK is never started and no data is sent to Google for ads.
  • Your GPS location, search text, photos and event history are never shared for advertising.
  • Google processes this data under its own policy and abroad: How Google uses data from partner apps. The transfer is based on your explicit consent (Turkish KVKK art. 9), which you can withdraw in Settings.

Sponsored suggestions: a business may pay to appear on at most one face of your die; that face is always labelled "Sponsored" and the die still rolls with equal odds.

Retention

  • Account, profile, points ledger, visit/attendance records, photos, business details and Tanış pre-registration declaration: as long as your account is open.
  • Location of places on your die: 24 hours. Google Maps coordinates in task records: at most 25 days (per Google Maps terms; place names are never stored).
  • Anonymous sessions: automatically deleted 30 days after creation.
  • Records of accepting the terms, giving/withdrawing consents and account events: 10 years (the general limitation period), so we can prove which text you accepted and how you gave your consents; kept for this period even if you delete your account, visible only to the authorised team and not used for any other purpose.
  • Server logs: contain no search text or location; kept for Google Cloud's default period (30 days).

Deleting your account

Settings → Account → delete my account permanently deletes your profile, nickname, points ledger, neighbourhood and visit records, attendance, reports, ad reward records, task records and photos; events you created are taken down and your businesses are unlinked from you. Data is removed from live systems immediately and from Google's backups within 30 days. Your terms/consent records and account events (including the deletion date) are excluded and kept for the period stated under "Retention".

Stored on your device

Last location, theme and settings, temporary service caches, suggestion preferences, on-device history and consent records (including an installation ID and the iOS identifier for vendor) are kept in your device's local storage. Deleting the app removes them; the server copy of your terms/consent records stays for the period stated under "Retention". These records are not shown in the app; you can request a copy at the contact address below.

Legal bases

Search, points, events and account features: performance of the service you request (KVKK art. 5/2-c); security, abuse prevention and moderation: legitimate interest (art. 5/2-f); keeping terms, consent and account event records: our legal obligations and the establishment, exercise or protection of a right (art. 5/2-ç and 5/2-e); ads and other optional purposes: your explicit consent (art. 5/1 and art. 9). The app is not offered in the European Union or mainland China App Stores.

Children

ZAR is not directed at children. To create an account you confirm you are over 13; if you are 13-17 a parent/guardian must consent. We delete accounts of children under 13 when we learn of them.

Your rights and contact

Under KVKK art. 11 you may learn whether your data is processed, request correction or deletion, object and withdraw consent. Requests: github.com/jakutp/zar/issues. Related policies: Google, Firebase, OSMF.
